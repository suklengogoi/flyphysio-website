Treatments

Hands-on care, backed by modern therapy

Every plan starts with a proper assessment, then a treatment matched to you.

Dry Needling

Fine needles release tight muscle knots and trigger points to ease deep, stubborn pain.

Cupping Therapy

Suction cups improve circulation and relax tight muscles in the neck, shoulders and back.

Laser Therapy

Low-level laser to calm inflammation and support healing in painful joints and soft tissue.

Electrotherapy

Pain-relieving electrical stimulation and machine-based therapy as part of your plan.

Orthopedic Rehab

Recovery after fractures, surgery and joint problems, with strength and mobility work.

Neuro Physiotherapy

Rehabilitation for nerve and brain-related weakness, balance and movement difficulty.

Good to know

Before your first visit

Have another question? Call us during clinic hours.

What should I bring?

Bring any previous prescriptions, X-rays, MRI or other reports you have, and wear loose, comfortable clothes so the painful area can be examined easily.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your condition and how long you have had it. After the first assessment, the doctor will explain the plan and what to expect.

Is dry needling or cupping painful?

Most people feel only mild, short-lived discomfort. The doctor will explain each treatment first and will only go ahead if you are comfortable.

Do you treat children and elderly patients?

Yes. The clinic treats pediatric and geriatric conditions along with orthopedic, neurological and sports problems.
